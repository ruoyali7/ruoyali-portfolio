# PRD research notes

## Product thesis

The strongest differentiation is the combination of professional 3D/architecture experience, software engineering, AI/LLM systems, and creative workflow automation. The site should repeatedly reinforce that bridge.

## What the PRD requires

- Primary identity: **Software Engineer & Creative Technologist**.
- Two job-search tracks: software engineering and 3D + AI + creative technology.
- Fast recruiter comprehension: positioning and evidence should be clear within 10–20 seconds.
- Curated case studies rather than a chronological archive.
- A consistent case-study template with explicit individual contribution and system/workflow diagrams.
- Real claims and real metrics only; concrete outcomes are acceptable when numerical impact is unavailable.
- Lightweight, accessible, responsive implementation with performance before decorative 3D effects.

## Main delivery risk

The PRD identifies a new AI × 3D project as necessary evidence. That is a content-production dependency, not merely a frontend task. The roadmap therefore separates content audit from site foundation and treats the project as its own phase.

## Scope guardrails for v1

Prioritize the homepage, Work index, flagship case studies, About, Resume, responsive behavior, accessibility, SEO, and performance. Defer heavy WebGL, role-targeted landing pages, AI portfolio guide, and other enhancements until the evidence-first v1 is complete.
